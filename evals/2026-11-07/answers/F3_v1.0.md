📍 SYNTHESIS

**1. Rao, N. Z. & Fuller, M. (2018). "Acidity and Antioxidant Activity of Cold Brew Coffee." *Scientific Reports*, 8, 16030.**
- Compared cold and hot brew made from several single-origin coffees.
- **pH was about the same** for both methods (roughly 4.85–5.13).
- **Titratable acidity was higher in hot brew.**
- Hot brew also had higher antioxidant activity.
- This supports "less acidic" only in the titratable-acidity sense, not in pH.

**2. Rao, N. Z., Fuller, M. & Grim, M. D. (2020). "Physiochemical Characteristics of Hot and Cold Brew Coffee Chemistry: The Effects of Roast Level and Brewing Temperature on Compound Extraction." *Foods*, 9(7), 902.**
- Compared hot and cold brew across different roast levels.
- Found that both roast level and brewing temperature affected extraction of acids and other compounds.
- As in the 2018 paper, pH differences were small.
- Treat the exact titratable-acidity results for each roast as unverified. Check them in the paper's tables before you quote them.
